IMPORTANT CAVEAT
================
This script samples VERTICES inside a tangent-plane disc around each
connector, not raycast surface hits. That's fine for understanding the
vertex distribution but misleading for "what a ray from outside hits."
The actuator_servo_high_torque GLB is one continuous primitive, so
vertices are scattered through the full body volume; the histogram
shows a wide `min..max` band not a tight "chamfer depth" signal.

Use this output to understand the MESH SHAPE and confirm the key facts
below. Use [icp][trace] logs at runtime for the real nudge numbers.

1. actuator_servo_high_torque.glb is a SINGLE primitive (10,542 verts,
   raw AABB [33.50, 58.50, 54.30] mm). Pre-axis-scale 1.388 × 0.615 × 0.534

3. structural_servo_coupler_disc.glb has rotation override [π/2, 0, 0]

4. actuator_servo_standard.glb has 16 primitives with a 12.18 mm shaft

5. Step 1 authored engagement_depth_mm=1.5 on actuator_servo_high_torque
   connector "top" (z=12, +Z axis). This is the SERVO BODY TOP, not the
   shaft tip. The semantic intent: when the servo mates body-top-down
   against a coupler, sink 1.5mm into the coupler so the chamfered

6. KNOWN BUG in Step 1: resolveMate() at mateConnectors.ts:227 reads
   ONLY the parent connector's engagement_depth_mm. When servo mates
   as the CHILD (coupler.bottom is parent, servo.top is child), the
   authored 1.5 mm on servo.top is silently dropped. This is the
   root cause the user sees in the quadruped — every hip/knee joint
   places servo as child on coupler.bottom (zero engagement).

7. Phase 1 log trace: every `[icp-nudge]` line in the user's smoke
   test was `skipped — parent … not in scene yet`. The in-loop hook
   reads from `ctx.getParsedRobot().linkGroups` but bulk assembly
   commits URDF text to a buffer and reparses ONCE at the end. During
   the loop the scene holds only `structural_baseplate_large_1`
   (reset-stub). Every deeper parent lookup returns undefined → skip.

CONCLUSION
==========

Step 2's no-op is caused by TWO independent bugs:

A. The runtime nudge ran inside the bulk-assembly loop against a scene
   that hadn't been reparsed. It skipped every mate.

B. Even if it had fired, the in-loop hook used the CACHED RAW GLB
   (servo_high_torque.glb) for the child, per-axis-scaled it to the
   preset bbox, and sampled it. That sampling produces a noisy "axial
   distribution" because the single GLB primitive has geometry all
   through its volume; the 90th-percentile pick would not land reliably
   on a "chamfer depth" scalar.

Phase 4 fix: move the nudge to a SCENE-LEVEL post-reconcile pass so
it runs against the LIVE rendered mesh (parent and child already
loaded + per-axis-scaled + shaft-overlay applied), with pivot-subtree
exclusion. Use raycasting against actual surfaces. Switch the metric
to "signed distance from child surface samples to parent surface,
measured along parent's connector axis" — matching the production
pattern from three research agents (Drake SignedDistancePair, Agent C
recommendation).

FOLLOW-UP NOTE (out of Step 2 scope)
====================================

The Step 1 child-side engagement_depth_mm bug (item 6 above) is not
fixed by ICP alone. Even with the ICP pass working, a well-authored
1.5 mm engagement on a CHILD connector still won't fire. That's a
Step 1 scope item — separate commit, separate workstream. ICP is
still the right fallback for IMPORTED (un-authored) components.
